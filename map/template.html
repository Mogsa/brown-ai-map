Brown University · AI and machine learning research, 2024–2026

Brown AI Research Map

Who works on what in AI and machine learning across Brown, built from every paper Brown AI faculty have published since January 2024. Each paper was read and assigned to a research area, and connections show faculty who publish together or co-advise students.

Draft, compiled from public data by a Brown CS ScM student. Paper lists come from OpenAlex, Google Scholar and Semantic Scholar, and areas were assigned by reading abstracts. There will be errors and gaps. Corrections are welcome and will be fixed.

Map of the research

Every paper, placed by what it is about

Each dot is a paper. Papers with similar abstracts sit close together, based on a language model trained on scientific papers (SPECTER). Positions come from the text alone. Colour shows the area each paper was assigned to, so where colours form clean regions, the areas match the research. Rings mark faculty, placed at the centre of their papers. Hover over a ring to see that person's papers; select an area to highlight it.

Zoom by pinching on a trackpad, Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, double-clicking, or the + and − buttons. Drag to move around.

"Neighbours in same area" is the share of each paper's 10 most similar papers that share its area (chance level in brackets).

  • Scope. Current Brown faculty whose research is mainly AI/ML, and papers whose version of record is dated 1 January 2024 or later (up to 29 September 2026).
  • Known gaps. Workshop papers and very new preprints are most likely to be missing. The five faculty added in September 2026 haven't had the Google Scholar check yet. Recent papers have had little time to be cited. Lab pages go stale: several listed students had graduated or left.
  • The areas are one reasonable division, not the only one. They were tested against the papers, but a different grouping would also be defensible.
  • No comparison with the wider field yet. A fair comparison would run Brown's papers and a sample of global papers through the same classifier.
